# Product decisions

- Product name is Luminaf and should remain easy to rename through metadata and UI constants.
- Near-black surfaces with mint/neon accents; avoid low-contrast neon body copy.
- Health calculations are estimates and must be labeled accordingly.
- Symptom guidance fails closed until a medically reviewed provider integration exists.
- Friends sharing is opt-in, mutual and limited to gamification summaries; health records remain private.
- Initial milestone establishes app shell and safety foundations. Integrations are staged rather than simulated.
